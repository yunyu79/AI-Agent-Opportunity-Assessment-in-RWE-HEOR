# Pain Point Definitions

1. Manual processing burden: Completing a task requires extensive manual operations, reading, or expert judgment, resulting in substantial human time investment.
2. Repetitive work: The same or similar information processing and operations are performed repeatedly.
3. Coordination and waiting: Tasks depend on clarification, handoffs, confirmation, or approval across individuals or teams, creating additional communication effort and waiting time that extend delivery timelines.
4. Errors and rework: Errors, omissions, or inconsistencies in data, logic, code, or outputs require additional checks, potentially including tracing back through the processes and results of multiple upstream steps.
5. Difficulty adapting and reusing existing work: In new projects, existing logic, code, or deliverables cannot be readily adapted or reused, requiring substantial modification, adaptation, and revalidation.
