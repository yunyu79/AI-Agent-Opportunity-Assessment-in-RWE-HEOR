# Stage 1 — Pain Point Evidence Log

Research date: 2026-10-29. Scope: retrospective observational RWE/HEOR workflows, including evidence synthesis and economic-model review.

**Evidence interpretation:** These sources establish workflow, reporting, or quality requirements. They generally do **not** measure real-world analyst labor, rework cost, frequency, or willingness to pay. Pain points are hypotheses unless directly measured. No scenario ranking is implied.

## Evidence grading and next actions

- **Guideline / reporting standard**: supports the existence or criticality of a requirement, not its duration or cost.
- **Tool documentation**: establishes supported check types or technical scope, not ROI or customer demand.
- **Observed operational pain**: requires task logs, comparative studies, or practitioner interviews.
- **Quantified ROI**: requires baseline time and cost, automation accuracy, human review time, remediation costs, and deployment expense.

For the next pass, extract direct measurement papers and vendor-independent benchmarks separately. Prioritize baseline hours per task, task frequency per study, error/rework incidence, and net hours after human review. Record a new evidence row for every independent study rather than overwriting these twelve foundational entries.
